Hiring Toolkit

Digital Marketing Interview

Interview questions, sample answers, a candidate scorecard, and a practical test for a Digital Marketing role.

Use the sample answers as a guide, not as a script the candidate must repeat word for word. Ask for real examples and evidence of practical work.

1. Digital Marketing Basics

Q1. What is digital marketing?

Sample answer

Digital marketing is the promotion of products or services through online channels such as social media, search engines, websites, email and paid advertisements.

Q2. What is the difference between SEO and SEM?

Sample answer

SEO improves a website's visibility in organic search results. SEM is search engine marketing and commonly includes paid search advertising.

Q3. Which digital marketing platforms and tools have you used?

Sample answer

Examples include Facebook, Instagram, Google Ads, Google Analytics, Canva, CapCut and Meta Business Suite. The candidate should explain which tools they have personally used and give a real example.

Q4. Explain reach, impressions and engagement.

Sample answer

Reach is the number of unique people who saw content. Impressions are the total number of times content was displayed. Engagement includes actions such as likes, comments, shares and saves.

2. Social Media Marketing

Q5. How would you promote a business on Facebook and Instagram?

Sample answer

Identify the target audience, create a content calendar, publish useful posts and Reels, respond to comments and messages, and review performance analytics.

Q6. How many posts should a business publish per week?

Sample answer

It depends on the business and audience. I would start with a consistent schedule, such as 3–5 quality posts per week, then adjust based on results and available resources.

Q7. What would you do if a page received very few likes and comments?

Sample answer

Review the audience, content quality, posting times and engagement data. Test different formats such as Reels, carousels and educational posts, then compare performance.

Q8. Which tools can you use to create posts and videos?

Sample answer

Canva for graphics, CapCut for video editing, and Meta Business Suite for scheduling and managing social media content.

3. Paid Advertising & Analytics

Q9. What is the difference between organic and paid marketing?

Sample answer

Organic marketing attracts an audience through unpaid content and search visibility. Paid marketing uses an advertising budget to reach selected audiences.

Q10. What is CTR?

Sample answer

CTR means Click-Through Rate. It is the percentage of impressions that result in clicks. Formula: CTR = (Clicks ÷ Impressions) × 100.

Q11. What is ROAS?

Sample answer

Return on Ad Spend measures revenue generated for each unit spent on advertising. For example, Rs. 40,000 in revenue from Rs. 10,000 ad spend is a 4x ROAS.

Q12. How would you use a Rs. 20,000 Facebook Ads budget?

Sample answer

Define the campaign objective, audience and expected result. Test creatives and audiences with part of the budget, monitor results, then move more budget toward better-performing ads while keeping the campaign goal in view.

4. Practical Marketing Scenarios

Q13. How would you generate leads for a website development company?

Sample answer

Identify businesses that need websites, publish useful content and case studies, run targeted campaigns, offer a demo or consultation, and follow up with leads through appropriate channels.

Q14. How would you market a Learning Management System (LMS) to schools?

Sample answer

Target school owners, principals and administrators. Create demo videos, highlight features such as student management, fees, results and online lessons, offer live demonstrations, and run campaigns to generate inquiries.

Q15. How do you measure whether a campaign is successful?

Sample answer

Measure performance against the campaign objective: leads, conversions, cost per lead, website traffic, engagement or return on ad spend. Report the results and suggest next steps.

Q16. Which AI tools can help with digital marketing?

Sample answer

ChatGPT can help with ideas and copywriting, Canva AI with design, and video or analytics tools with production and reporting. Review AI output for accuracy, brand fit and originality before publishing.

6. Practical Test (15–20 minutes)

  1. Write the content for one Instagram post for a business.
  2. Create a headline and description for one Facebook or Instagram ad.
  3. Identify the target audience for a website development service.
  4. Prepare a simple plan for a Rs. 10,000 advertising budget.
  5. Explain how you would evaluate campaign performance after seven days.

Hiring tip: Do not select a candidate based only on memorized answers. Ask for real work samples, practical results and an explanation of their own contribution.
